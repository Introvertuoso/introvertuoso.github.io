<p style="text-align: justify;">
I'm a first-year PhD candidate at the <a href='https://cispa.de' target='_blank'>Helmholtz Center CISPA</a>, supervised by <a href='https://vreeken.eu' target='_blank'>Jilles Vreeken</a>, and a member of the <a href='https://www.neuroexplicit.org' target='_blank'>Research Training Group on Neuroexplicit Models</a>. I am interested in working on explainable and interpretable machine learning and data mining methods, particularly for the BioMed domain.
<br><br>
Previously, I obtained my Master of Science in <a href='https://saarland-informatics-campus.de/en/studium-studies/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-master/' target='_blank'>Data Science and Artificial Intelligence</a> from <a href='https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/home.html' target='_blank'>Saarland University</a>. I wrote my Master's thesis on differentiable subgroup discovery in time-to-event (survival) data with the <a href='https://eda.rg.cispa.io' target='_blank'>Exploratory Data Analysis</a> group. In 2021, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Information Technology Engineering from <a href='https://www.damascusuniversity.edu.sy/index.php?lang=2' target='_blank'>Damascus University</a>. For my graduation project, I worked on a computer-vision-powered system that detects security- and safety-related events.
