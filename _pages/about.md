<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am a second-year computer science Ph.D. candidate at the <a href='https://cispa.de/en/' target='_blank'>Helmholtz Center CISPA</a>, advised by <a href='https://vreeken.eu' target='_blank'>Jilles Vreeken</a>, and a member of the <a href='https://www.neuroexplicit.org' target='_blank'>Neuroexplicit Models Research Training Group</a>. My research focuses on explainable and interpretable machine learning and data mining methods, with a primary application in biomedicine.
<br><br>
Previously, I obtained my M.Sc. in <a href='https://saarland-informatics-campus.de/en/studium-studies/data-science-and-artificial-intelligence-master/' target='_blank'>Data Science and Artificial Intelligence</a> from <a href='https://saarland-informatics-campus.de/en/' target='_blank'>Saarland University</a>. I wrote my Master's thesis on differentiable subgroup discovery in time-to-event (survival) data with the <a href='https://eda.rg.cispa.io' target='_blank'>Exploratory Data Analysis</a> group. Before that, I earned my B.Eng. in Information Technology from <a href='https://www.damascusuniversity.edu.sy/index.php?lang=2' target='_blank'>Damascus University</a>, where for my graduation project I developed a computer-vision-based event detection system for safety and security.
