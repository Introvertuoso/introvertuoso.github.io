In *In Vitro* Fertilization (IVF) and Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART), precise timing of oocyte retrieval following ovulation induction is a critical determinant of treatment success. Following the administration of recombinant human chorionic gonadotropin (rhCG, Ovitrelle®) to trigger final oocyte maturation, identifying the optimal time window for retrieval is essential for maximizing clinical pregnancy rates.

In our cohort study published in *BMC Pregnancy and Childbirth*, **["The optimal period for oocyte retrieval after the administration of recombinant human chorionic gonadotropin in in vitro fertilization"](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12884-022-04412-9)**, we analyzed clinical records across **3,362 couples** to investigate the optimal timing for oocyte retrieval.

---

## Study Cohort & Methodology

* **Study Design:** Retrospective cohort study.
* **Patient Cohort:** Digital records of **3,362 Middle Eastern couples** who underwent IVF treatment between 2019 and 2021.
* **Ovulation Trigger:** Recombinant human chorionic gonadotropin (rhCG) (Ovitrelle®).
* **Protocols Evaluated:** Controlled ovarian stimulation protocols, with specific evaluation of the gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonist (GnRHa) long protocol.
* **Primary Outcome:** Clinical pregnancy occurrence.

---

## Statistical Analysis & Findings

1. **Positive Correlation Up to Hour 37:** Statistical testing identified a significant positive correlation between the elapsed oocyte retrieval period and clinical pregnancy occurrence up to the **37th hour**.
2. **Optimal Timing at Hour 37:** Retrieval at the **37th hour** post-trigger was found to provide the most optimal clinical pregnancy outcome.
3. **Protocol Association:** This benefit was especially pronounced in patients undergoing the **gonadotropin-releasing hormone agonist (GnRHa) long protocol**.
4. **Clinical Recommendation:** Based on the cohort findings, the study recommends scheduling oocyte retrieval at **hour 37** following ovulation triggering under the described clinical conditions.
